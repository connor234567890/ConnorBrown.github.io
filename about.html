About This Newsletter

This newsletter focuses on applied artificial intelligence in gaming, including AI-driven NPCs, game development tools, and player-facing AI systems.

I chose this topic because AI is rapidly changing how games are built and experienced. From immersive character interactions to faster development workflows, AI is becoming a practical tool rather than a novelty.

This site documents those changes while exploring both the opportunities and challenges that come with integrating AI into modern games.
